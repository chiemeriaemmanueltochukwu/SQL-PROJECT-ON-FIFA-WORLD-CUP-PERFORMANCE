# FIFA Analyst Questions

These are portfolio-ready analytical questions. The SQL returns the actual warehouse results when run against the PostgreSQL project.

1. Who are the top 10 tournament goal scorers?
2. Who are the top 10 assist providers?
3. Which players have the highest tournament ratings?
4. Which teams scored the most goals?
5. Which players accumulated the most minutes?
6. Which teams conceded the most goals?
7. What is the relationship between goals and assists at player level?
8. How do player ratings vary by position?

## Important
The repository deliberately does not contain invented player statistics. Export the query results from PostgreSQL and use the chart scripts in `analysis/` to create the PNG visuals.
